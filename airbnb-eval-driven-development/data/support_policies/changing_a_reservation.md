# Changing a Reservation

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "How to change a reservation" and related trip-change
articles (https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/913, https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1505).

## Requesting a change (guest)

To change the dates, number of guests, or other details of a confirmed home
reservation, a guest submits a **trip change request** to the host. From Trips,
select the trip, open Your stay, and under Reservation details choose Change
reservation, make the changes, and send the request. The host then receives a
notification to approve or decline it.

## Proposing a change (host)

To change a future or current reservation, the host sends the guest a trip change
request proposing the new dates or price. The change takes effect only if the
other party accepts it.

## Pricing and payment

Before a trip change request is submitted, both the original and the new total
cost are shown. If the new cost differs, the necessary adjustment — a charge or a
refund — is processed when the request is accepted:

- In most cases the guest's original payment method is charged automatically.
- For some payment methods, the guest is asked to submit payment within **48
  hours**.

A trip change request must be accepted by the other party before any new dates,
guest count, or price takes effect; nothing changes while the request is pending.
