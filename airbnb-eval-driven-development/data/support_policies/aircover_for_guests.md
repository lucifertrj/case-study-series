# AirCover for Guests

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "AirCover for guests" (https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3218)
and "What happens if my Host cancels my reservation" (https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/170).

Every home booking comes with AirCover for guests at no extra cost. If there is a
serious issue with the Airbnb that the host cannot resolve, Airbnb will help the
guest get rebooked or provide a full or partial refund.

## What is covered

AirCover for guests applies when:

- the host cancels before check-in;
- the guest cannot check in or cannot get in touch with the host;
- the listing is significantly different than advertised and the host cannot fix
  it — for example, the heating does not work in winter, there are fewer bedrooms
  than listed, the home type is wrong (a private room instead of an entire home),
  or a major advertised amenity such as a pool or kitchen is missing;
- the guest feels unsafe.

Minor inconveniences — such as a broken toaster — are not covered.

## How to report a travel issue

1. **Contact the host first.** Notify the host about the problem within **72 hours
   of discovering it** and give them a chance to resolve it.
2. **Document the issue.** Take photos or videos as evidence.
3. If the host cannot resolve it, contact Airbnb to escalate the claim.

## Remedies

- If a comparable place is available, Airbnb helps the guest rebook, based on
  availability and comparable pricing, at no additional cost to the guest.
- If a comparable place is not available, or the guest prefers not to rebook,
  Airbnb provides a full or partial refund, **including service fees**.

## If the host cancels

If a host cancels before check-in, the guest gets a full refund, including service
fees, if they choose not to rebook. Eligible guests may receive Airbnb booking
credit immediately so they can rebook right away; if that credit is not used
within 72 hours, it automatically converts to a full refund instead. The refund is
issued to the original payment method and is initiated immediately, though the
time to arrive depends on the guest's bank or financial institution. Airbnb sends
an email with full details and refund information.

If a host cancels after check-in, the guest receives a full or partial refund plus
rebooking assistance, depending on the circumstances.

Guests should never cancel on a host's behalf — the host must initiate the
cancellation for the guest to receive a full refund.
