# Cancellation Policies and Refunds

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "Cancellation policies for your stay"
(https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/475). Figures reflect the policy text as
published; exact amounts can vary by reservation and region.

## 24-hour cancellation period (standard policies, stays under 28 nights)

All standard cancellation policies include a 24-hour cancellation period: a guest
can cancel for a full refund, including all taxes, within 24 hours of booking, as
long as the reservation was confirmed at least 7 days before check-in.

## Standard policies (stays of 27 nights or fewer)

### Flexible
- Cancel up until 24 hours before check-in: full refund of the nightly rate plus
  taxes.
- Cancel within 24 hours of check-in: taxes are refunded (pro-rated); the host is
  paid for each night the guest stays plus one additional night.

### Moderate
- Cancel up until 5 days before check-in: full refund plus taxes.
- Cancel within 5 days of check-in: all taxes refunded; the host is paid one
  night plus 50% of the remaining nights.
- Cancel after check-in: pro-rated taxes refunded; the host is paid for each night
  stayed, one additional night, plus 50% of the remaining nights.

### Limited (bookings made on or after October 1, 2025)
- Cancel up until 14 days before check-in: full refund plus taxes.
- Cancel 7 to 14 days before check-in: 50% refund of the nightly rate plus full
  taxes; the host keeps 50%.
- Cancel less than 7 days before check-in: taxes refunded only; the host keeps
  100%.
- Cancel after check-in: no refund of the nightly rate; the host keeps 100%.

### Firm
- Cancel up until 30 days before check-in: full refund plus taxes.
- Cancel 7 to 30 days before check-in: 50% refund of the nightly rate plus full
  taxes; the host keeps 50%.
- Cancel less than 7 days before check-in: taxes refunded only; the host keeps
  100%.
- Cancel after check-in: no refund of the nightly rate; the host keeps 100%.

### Strict (invitation only)
- Cancel 7 or more days before check-in: 50% refund of the nightly rate plus full
  taxes.
- Cancel less than 7 days before check-in: taxes refunded only; the host keeps
  100%.
- Cancel after check-in: no refund of the nightly rate.

### Super Strict 30 Days / Super Strict 60 Days (invitation only)
- Cancel 30 (or 60) or more days before check-in: 50% refund of the nightly rate
  plus full taxes.
- Cancel less than 30 (or 60) days before check-in: taxes refunded only; the host
  keeps 100%.
- Cancel after check-in: no refund of the nightly rate.

## Long-term stays (28 nights or more)

### Firm (long-term)
- Cancel 30 or more days before check-in: full refund plus taxes.
- Cancel after that: taxes refunded; the host is paid for each night stayed plus
  the next 30 nights (or, if fewer than 30 nights remain, the remaining nights).
- Cancel after check-in: no refund of the nightly rate.

### Strict (long-term)
- Cancel within 48 hours of booking AND at least 28 days before check-in: full
  refund plus taxes.
- Cancel after that: taxes refunded; the host is paid for nights stayed plus the
  next 30 nights (or the remaining nights if fewer than 30 remain).
- Cancel after check-in: no refund of the nightly rate.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the payment method used at booking. Most refunds arrive
within 15 days, though the exact timing depends on the payment method and region.
