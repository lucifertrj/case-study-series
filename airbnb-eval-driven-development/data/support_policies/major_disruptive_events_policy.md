# Major Disruptive Events Policy

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "What is the Major Disruptive Events Policy?"
(https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1320). This policy was previously called the
Extenuating Circumstances Policy. The current policy applies to all trips and
Experiences taking place on or after June 6, 2024, regardless of when they were
booked.

## When the policy applies

The policy may apply when a large-scale event occurs after the time of booking and
prevents or legally prohibits completion of a future or ongoing reservation.

## Covered events

- **Government-declared public health emergencies**: epidemics, pandemics, and
  public health emergencies (this excludes endemic diseases and COVID-19).
- **Government travel restrictions**: mandatory restrictions imposed by a
  government agency, such as an evacuation order or shelter-in-place order (this
  excludes non-binding travel advisories or other government guidance).
- **Military actions and hostilities**: acts of war, hostilities, invasions, civil
  war, terrorism, explosions, bombings, rebellions, riots, and insurrection.
- **Large-scale utility outages**: prolonged outages of essential utilities such
  as heat, water, and electricity that impact the vast majority of homes in a
  location.
- **Natural disasters and severe weather**: unforeseeable natural disasters like
  earthquakes and tsunamis, and unforeseeable severe weather events like
  tornadoes (this excludes weather or natural conditions that are foreseeable for
  that location).

## What is NOT covered

- Personal emergencies such as unexpected injury or illness.
- Government obligations such as jury duty.
- Non-binding travel advisories or other government guidance.
- Cancellation of an event for which the reservation was made.
- Transportation disruptions such as airline insolvency, flight cancellations, or
  transportation strikes.
- Foreseeable weather patterns for the location.

## What happens when the policy applies

When the policy applies, eligible guests can cancel and receive a refund, travel
credit, and/or other consideration regardless of the reservation's cancellation
policy. Covered hosts can cancel without fees or other adverse consequences,
though the dates are blocked on their calendar and they receive no payout.
