# Service Fees

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "Why are there service fees?"
(https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1857).

## Guest service fee

Guests pay a service fee that ranges from **14.1% to 16.5% of the booking
subtotal**. The booking subtotal includes the nightly price and any additional
fees charged by the host (such as a cleaning fee), but excludes the guest service
fee and taxes.

The exact percentage varies based on a variety of factors and may be higher or
lower for a given booking. For cross-currency bookings — where the guest pays in a
different currency from the one the host set for the listing — the guest service
fee includes an additional amount, resulting in a guest service fee of up to 16.5%
of the booking subtotal.

The guest service fee is shown in the **Price breakdown** before a reservation is
booked.

## Host service fee

Airbnb has two host-fee structures:

- **Split-fee structure** (the default for most listings): most hosts pay a **3%**
  host service fee, deducted from the payout. Hosts pay a **4%** fee for listings
  in Brazil and Mexico. Under this structure the guest pays a separate guest
  service fee (the 14.1%–16.5% above).
- **Single-fee (host-only) structure**: the entire fee is deducted from the host's
  payout and no separate guest service fee is charged. Most hosts on this
  structure pay **15.5%**; the remainder typically pay **14%–16%**, and hosts pay
  **16%** for listings in Brazil and Mexico.
