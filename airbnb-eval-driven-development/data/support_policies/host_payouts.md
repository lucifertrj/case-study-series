# Host Payouts

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "When you'll get your payout"
(https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/92) and "Payout method processing times"
(https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/425).

## When a payout is released

For a home reservation, the payout is released about 24 hours after the guest's
scheduled check-in time. Hosts of experiences or services are paid after the
experience or service is completed.

In some cases the payout is not released until 1 business day after checkout — for
example when a new host has not verified a listing, when a host has breached the
terms or policies, or when Airbnb believes it is reasonably necessary to protect
Airbnb, its members, or third parties. For a first-time host, Airbnb may hold the
payout until 30 days after the reservation was confirmed.

A host must have a valid payout method set up before a payout can be sent.

## Long-term stays (28 nights or more)

The initial payout is released 24 hours after the guest's scheduled check-in. The
payments for subsequent nights are then collected and released every month (every
28–31 days) following the initial payout for the rest of the reservation.

## How long each payout method takes

Once a payout is released by Airbnb, the time to receive the money depends on the
payout method:

- **Fast Pay**: 30 minutes or less, available in the US only. A 1.5% fee is
  deducted from each payout, up to a maximum of $15 USD (tax included).
- **PayPal**: within 1 business day.
- **ACH / direct deposit**: 3 business days, available for the USA and Puerto Rico
  only.
- **Bank transfer (to a bank account)**: 3–5 business days.
- **International wire**: 3–7 business days.
- **Payoneer Prepaid Debit Mastercard**: within 24 hours.
- **Western Union**: 1 business day, depending on country or region.

## Payout method setup / verification times

A newly added payout method must be verified before it can be used:

- Bank account: 3–5 business days to be ready.
- ACH / direct deposit: 5–7 business days to be ready (USA and Puerto Rico only).
- PayPal, international wire, and Western Union: up to 2 business days to be ready.

## Refunds and payout adjustments

If a host cancels a reservation, or another reservation issue disrupts the guest's
stay, the host receives no payout or has the payout reduced by the amount refunded
to the guest.
