# Reviews

Source: Airbnb Help Center — "Writing reviews" and related review articles
(https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/367, https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/13).

## The 14-day review window

On the day of checkout, both the guest and the host are prompted to leave a
review. Both parties have **14 days after checkout** to submit their review.

## When reviews are published

Reviews are posted only after both parties have submitted their reviews, or once
the 14-day review period ends — whichever comes first. Neither party can see the
other's review until both are published.

## Editing a review

For homes, if you submit your review first, you can edit it any time within the
14-day review period, up until the other party submits their review. Once both
reviews are submitted, or the 14-day period ends (whichever comes first), both
reviews are published automatically and no further changes can be made. Reviews
for services and experiences cannot be edited.

## After publication

A published review stays on Airbnb unless the author requests its removal within
**30 days of publication**, the review violates Airbnb's policy on authentic and
trustworthy reviews, or other circumstances affect its visibility.
